# Decisions

## 2026-07-29 — Context compiler prepares; coding harness plans

- Holodeck compiles exact task, authority, evidence, repository, and workspace
  context. Codex, Claude Code, or another harness may propose the technical
  implementation plan.
- A harness plan is attributable generated output, not a binding mission,
  approval, requirement, or acceptance decision.

## 2026-07-29 — Interpretations are task-local and four-valued

- M3 may generate task-local hypotheses over M2 facts.
- Probe status is `activated`, `cleared`, `unresolved`, or `conflicted`.
- `Cleared` requires a bounded evidence argument. Empty search results or
  missing graph edges produce `unresolved`.
- The first probes are data/state/contracts and authority/security/control.
  Broader dimensions require benchmark evidence.

## 2026-07-29 — Minimum sufficient context

- The M2 graph is an internal navigation layer and is never dumped wholesale
  into a prompt.
- Mandatory instructions and requirements are preserved; references are ranked
  and bounded; omitted items retain reasons and retrieval handles.
- Context expansion creates a linked immutable supplement, never a silent
  mutation of the initial packet.
