# Gates

## Intake

- The task links the M2 handoff and governing product/context specifications.
- Scope, non-goals, authority, inputs, outputs, and failure behavior are clear.

## Readiness

- M2 snapshot/query schemas and coverage semantics are accepted and pinned.
- Benchmark arms, metrics, budgets, repetitions, and promotion thresholds are
  registered before interpretive experiments.
- Literal human input, repository facts, approved knowledge, and generated
  hypotheses have separate record types and trust.

## Implementation

- Identical inputs/revisions/configuration produce identical packet structure
  and content hash.
- No source text or model output can silently become instruction authority.
- Missing evidence is unresolved, not cleared.
- Every inclusion, exclusion, hypothesis, and omission is explainable.
- The compiler does not launch a worker or approve its own mission.

## Verification

- Tests cover ambiguity, authority, tenant isolation, stale/partial context,
  budget truncation, replay, role differences, packet hashing, expansion, and
  harness failures.
- The benchmark compares ordinary search, cheap sentinels, factual graph, and
  interpretive probes under equal conditions.

## Done

- Acceptance criteria and exact commands/results are recorded.
- Changed artifacts and residual risks are listed.
- Board index, packet, lane, and updates agree.
- M4 receives an implementation-independent mission/context handoff.
